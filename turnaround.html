Questions before you book

What the Turnaround is. And what it isn’t.

Won’t this add bureaucracy and slow delivery?

The controls fit around live delivery. The aim is fewer rework cycles, because change is priced before it starts, not after.

Will this die when you leave?

That’s why the handover is a core deliverable. Templates, standards and meeting scripts are built to run without us.

We already do governance. What’s different?

Change doesn’t enter without a ticket. Dates don’t move without an impact conversation. Decisions don’t get made outside the forum.

Six weeks seems short.

It installs the system on one live programme, not the whole organisation. By week six your team owns the rhythm.

What happens after the six weeks?

Your team runs the system. If you want sustained oversight, the CONTROL Tower retainer keeps the cadence and commercial defence in place. There is no forced retainer.
