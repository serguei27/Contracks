Delivery control on a cross-border energy programme

Situation
A cross-border EPC programme connecting two national energy systems: multiple contractors, multiple jurisdictions, and the scale at which a single unmanaged gap cascades fast.
Intervention
One operating truth across contractors and borders, decisions closed before they could cascade, vendor commitments verified, on a fixed control cadence.
Outcome
Issues exposed early, while still manageable. Commitments, decisions and cross-border interfaces were tracked before they became board-level surprises.
