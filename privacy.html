Privacy notice

How we use your details.

Who we are. Contracks Global SRL (Belgium) is the controller of the personal data you send through this site.

What we collect. The details you enter in our forms. To request a call: name, company, email, your preferred contact method and the WhatsApp number, phone number or LinkedIn profile for it, when best to contact you and your message. To unlock your Delivery Score report: your email and, if you give it, your name. When you unlock the report or request a call after taking the Delivery Score, we also receive your score, your answers to its questions and the date and version of the score. Your Delivery Score answers aren’t sent to us unless you unlock the report or request a call. We never put your contact details or answers in a web address. To apply to join our network: your name, email, expertise, your video link and your slides or a link to them.

Why. To reply to your request, arrange and prepare your call, give you your report and, if you go ahead, prepare a proposal. Giving your email for the report doesn’t subscribe you to anything. If you tick the box when you unlock your report, we may also send the occasional field note on delivery control, and you can unsubscribe at any time. When you register for the library, we use your name and email to give you access and to know who is reading. If you tick the box, we may also email you news from Contracks Global, and you can unsubscribe at any time. We don’t sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing.

Where it’s stored. Form submissions are processed by our hosting provider, Netlify, and delivered to our mailbox. Our email and contact tools are provided by Zoho, and automatic emails by Resend.

How long. Delivery Score submissions and call requests are kept for 12 months, then deleted, unless we are working together. Other details are kept only as long as needed for these purposes, then deleted. You can ask us to delete yours sooner at any time.

Your rights. You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, at any time. Email . You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority.
