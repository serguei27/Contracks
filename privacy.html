Privacy notice

How we use your details.

Who we are. Contracks Global SRL (Belgium) is the controller of the personal data you send through this site.

What we collect. The details you enter in our forms: name, company, email, phone, location, information about your programme and your comments. If you request a diagnostic report, we also receive your diagnostic score and context answers. Your diagnostic answers aren’t sent to us unless you request the report.

Why. To reply to your request, book and prepare your call, send your report and, if you go ahead, prepare a proposal. When you request a report, we may also send the occasional field note on delivery control, and you can unsubscribe at any time. When you register for the library, we use your name and email to give you access and to know who is reading. If you tick the box, we may also email you news from Contracks Global, and you can unsubscribe at any time. We don’t sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing.

Where it’s stored. Form submissions are processed by our hosting provider, Netlify, and delivered to our mailbox. Our email and contact tools are provided by Zoho, and automatic emails by Resend. The results page can show a video from YouTube.

How long. Only as long as needed for these purposes, then deleted.

Your rights. You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, at any time. Email hello@contracks.global. You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority.
